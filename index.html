Matthew Hanna

Born in 2003 in Melbourne, Australia.

Matthew Hanna is an artist based in Melbourne and Geelong, Victoria. He is drawn to the spaces people move through and the quiet ways a place can change how we feel.

His work is shaped by an interest in sustainability, a care for materials and a commitment to seeing an idea through from first sketch to finished piece.

He shares his process and influences online, where a growing audience follows along.
